Fitters Black Book

Your workshop. Your knowledge. One place.

Fitters Black Book is a practical digital reference platform designed for fitters and workshop professionals across a range of industries and trades.

What’s included

* The Library – Organised technical knowledge across 10 categories.
* Workshop Tools – Useful everyday calculations and conversions.
* Thread Charts – Quick-reference thread information.
* My Bench – Save and pin frequently used references.
* Personalisation – Tailor your experience to your role, units and work areas.
* Membership – Basic, Pro Monthly and Pro Annual membership options.

Membership Plans

* Basic – Free forever
* Pro Monthly – $9.99 AUD/month
* Pro Annual – $79 AUD/year

Pro plans are planned to include a 7-day free trial.

Project Status

Fitters Black Book is currently a prototype.

User accounts, online payments, subscription management, trial activation and cloud synchronisation are not yet connected.

Technical reference information and calculations should always be checked against applicable standards, manufacturer specifications and engineering requirements.
